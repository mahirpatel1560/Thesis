## 1. Business

Apple designs, manufactures and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables and accessories, and sells related services [10-K 1]. The hardware lines are iPhone (based on iOS), Mac (macOS), iPad (iPadOS), and Wearables, Home and Accessories, which includes Apple Watch, AirPods/Beats, Apple Vision Pro, Apple TV 4K and HomePod [10-K 1]. Services span advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content and the App Store, subscription services (Apple Arcade, Fitness+, Music, News+, TV), and payment services (Apple Card, Apple Pay) [10-K 1].

Customers are primarily in consumer, small/mid-sized business, education, enterprise and government markets [10-K 1]. In 2025, direct channels (retail, online, direct sales) accounted for 40% of net sales and indirect channels (carriers, resellers) 60% [10-K 1]. The company reports on a geographic basis: Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan and Rest of Asia Pacific [10-K 1].

Revenue by category in FY2025: iPhone $209.59B, Services $109.16B, Mac $33.71B, Wearables/Home/Accessories $35.69B, iPad $28.02B [10-K 7]. By geography: Americas $178.35B, Europe $111.03B, Greater China $64.38B, Japan $28.70B, Rest of Asia Pacific $33.70B [10-K 7]. The company had approximately 166,000 full-time equivalent employees as of September 27, 2025 [10-K 1].

## 2. Moat

The company designs and develops nearly the entire solution for its products — hardware, operating system, software applications and related services [10-K 1]. It holds a broad portfolio of patents, designs, copyrights, trademarks and trade secrets and is pursuing thousands of applications worldwide, though it states no single intellectual property right is solely responsible for protecting its products [10-K 1].

Evidence of pricing power and ecosystem strength shows in margins: total gross margin percentage rose to 46.9% in FY2025 from 46.2% in FY2024 and 44.1% in FY2023, driven by Services gross margin of 75.4% [10-K 7]. Services net sales grew 14% to $109.16B in FY2025, with growth from advertising, App Store and cloud services [10-K 7]. Third-party developer support is described as a factor in hardware purchase decisions, creating an ecosystem tie-in [10-K 1A].

Honest limits: the company itself states it has a minority market share in the global smartphone, personal computer, tablet and wearables markets [10-K 1]. It describes its markets as highly competitive with aggressive price competition and downward pressure on gross margins, and notes competitors imitate its product features and IP [10-K 1].

## 4. Risks

- **Concentrated overseas manufacturing:** A significant majority of manufacturing is done by outsourcing partners located primarily in China mainland, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Vietnam, with final assembly of substantially all hardware in Asia — reducing direct control over cost, quality and quantity [10-K 1A].
- **Tariffs and trade disputes:** Beginning in Q2 2025, new U.S. tariffs were announced on imports from China, India, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam and the EU, with reciprocal measures from other countries and a Section 232 semiconductor investigation underway; the impact is uncertain and can hit supply chain, pricing and gross margin [10-K 1A][10-K 7]. Tariff costs already pressured Products gross margin percentage in FY2025 [10-K 7].
- **Single/limited-source components:** Many critical components come from single or limited sources, exposing the company to shortages and price increases; new products often use custom components from only one source [10-K 1A].
- **Constant product-transition execution:** The company must continually introduce new products and manage transitions; new offerings may produce lower revenues and lower profit margins, and failure to manage this hurts results [10-K 1A].
- **Intense competition and minority market share:** Markets feature aggressive price cutting, competitors willing to operate at little/no profit, and imitation of Apple's features and IP; the company holds minority share across its main hardware categories [10-K 1A].
- **Product/software defects, including AI features:** Complex hardware/software can have defects; the introduction of AI features can increase safety risks including exposing users to harmful or inaccurate content [10-K 1A].
- **Dependence on third-party developers and content:** Hardware demand depends partly on availability of third-party apps; minority market share can make developers less inclined to build for Apple's platforms [10-K 1A]. Content licensing arrangements may not renew on reasonable terms [10-K 1A].
- **Third-party IP access:** Some products require licensed third-party IP; failure to obtain it on reasonable terms can force product modifications; AI use of copyrighted training material may raise infringement risk [10-K 1A].
- **Macroeconomic sensitivity:** A majority of net sales are outside the U.S.; recession, inflation, higher rates and currency fluctuations can reduce demand [10-K 1A]. Foreign-currency weakness had an unfavorable effect on Americas net sales in FY2025 [10-K 7].

## 6. Bull case / Bear case

**Bull:**
- Revenue re-accelerated to +6% in FY2025 ($416.16B), and reported ttm revenue growth is 16.6% [10-K 7][yf valuation].
- Services is the fastest-growing, highest-margin segment: +14% to $109.16B at 75.4% gross margin, lifting total gross margin to 46.9% [10-K 7].
- Strong cash generation: $98.77B FCF, $132.4B cash and marketable securities, and $89.3B of buybacks plus $15.4B dividends in FY2025 with a new $100B repurchase authorization [yf financials][10-K 7].
- Diversified geographic growth outside China: Japan +15%, Europe +10%, Rest of Asia Pacific +10%, Americas +7% [10-K 7].
- Integrated hardware/software/services model and large IP portfolio support pricing power [10-K 1].

**Bear:**
- Valuation sits at the top of the 52-week range after a 57.6% one-year run, with trailing P/E 39.7 and EV/EBITDA 30.2 — richer than both listed peers on those metrics, despite lower revenue growth [yf prices][yf valuation][yf peers].
- FY2025 net income growth was aided by a lower effective tax rate (15.6% vs 24.1%), including a $10.7B benefit tied to the State Aid Decision — a factor that may not recur [10-K 7].
- Greater China net sales fell 4% (to $64.38B) on lower iPhone sales [10-K 7].
- Heavy manufacturing concentration in Asia plus active U.S. tariffs already pressured Products gross margin percentage (36.8% vs 37.2%) in FY2025 [10-K 7][10-K 1A].
- Concentration in iPhone: $209.59B of $416.16B total (about half of revenue) [10-K 7], and the company holds only minority market share in its hardware categories [10-K 1].

## 7. What would kill this thesis

- **Tariff escalation hitting margins:** Further U.S. tariffs or an adverse Section 232 semiconductor outcome that pushes total gross margin percentage below the FY2025 level of 46.9% or Products gross margin below the FY2025 36.8% [10-K 7][10-K 1A].
- **China deterioration:** Continued decline in Greater China net sales beyond the FY2025 −4% ($64.38B) figure [10-K 7].
- **iPhone stall:** iPhone net sales (+4% in FY2025 to $209.59B) turning negative, given its ~50% revenue share [10-K 7].
- **Services deceleration:** Services growth (+14% in FY2025) slowing materially or its 75.4% gross margin compressing [10-K 7].
- **Tax normalization:** Effective tax rate rising back toward the FY2024 24.1% (from FY2025 15.6%), which would pressure net income even at flat pre-tax income [10-K 7].
- **Capital return pullback:** Buyback pace falling well below the FY2025 $89.3B or a pause in the $100B authorization [10-K 7].
- **Litigation/regulatory events:** Watch news items such as "Apple sues OpenAI" [news: AI Weekly: SK Hynix shares jump up and down, Apple sues OpenAI] and any binding rulings that force product modifications or IP-sharing [10-K 1A].
